Modals & Dialogs

Dialogs and modals are essential components in Superdesk, used frequently for everything from quick confirmations to complex workflows. Given the sophisticated nature of content management, our modals often contain substantial functionality. Consistent structure and content guidelines help users navigate these interfaces confidently.

Structure

Every dialog consists of three main areas: Header (with title), Content, and Footer. The Header and Content are always required, while the Footer is recommended in most cases.

Header

Purpose

Contains the dialog title and close button. Provides immediate context about the dialog's purpose.

Requirements

  • Title is always mandatory
  • Close (×) button aligned to the right is mandatory in most cases
  • Close button can only be removed when demanding a clear action from the user in the footer

Content

Purpose

The main area for information, forms, or other interactive elements that support the dialog's goal.

Requirements

  • Always mandatory
  • Should clearly explain what's happening or what the user needs to do

Footer

Purpose

Contains action buttons that allow users to proceed, cancel, or complete the dialog's purpose.

Requirements

  • Recommended in most cases but can be skipped occasionally
  • Rare exception: large and complex modals where actions happen inside the content area
  • Main action buttons always aligned to the right
  • Primary action positioned far right

Content Guidelines

Clear Titles

Use specific, descriptive titles that immediately tell users what the dialog is about.

Do

  • "Save changes before closing?"
  • "Export Customer Data"
  • "You have unsaved changes"

Don't

  • "Are you sure?"
  • "Confirmation"
  • "Dialog"

Explicit Button Labels

Button text should clearly state what will happen when clicked, not generic actions.

Do

  • "Delete Profile"
  • "Save & Continue"
  • "Discard Changes"

Don't

  • "Ignore"
  • "Submit"
  • "Yes"

Clear Content

Explain what's happening and what the outcome will be. Users should never guess what an action will do.

Do

  • "This will permanently delete the profile and all its data. This action cannot be undone."
  • "Your changes will be saved and you'll return to the article."

Don't

  • "This action will affect your data."
  • "Proceed with the operation?"
